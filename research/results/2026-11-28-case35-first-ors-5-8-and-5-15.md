## A concrete 160-clause lower bound in Lean

PlanePrefixClauseLowerBound states the lower bound for the actual input bit masks and the fixed 17-gate prefix. It quantifies over any number of arbitrary clauses, each a list of natural-number indices into the source signature. The signature contains the 43 raw input/prefix wires and their 43 copies masked by wire 32. For every clause family that accepts the 38 selected positives and rejects the 160 selected negatives, the theorem proves that its size is at least 160. The conclusion concerns the number of OR clauses in a conjunction.

The proof recomputes the selected row signatures from the prefix. For each pair of distinct negatives, six bits in a witness table choose a positive whose true bits are covered by the union of the pair's true bits. Lean checks this relation for every pair by ordinary kernel computation. Any clause accepting the witness must accept one of the negatives. Assigning one rejecting clause to each negative is therefore injective. The row tables, witness table and complete proof are in the single Lean file. There are 12,720 unordered pairs.
